Title: Android Security and Countersurveillance With GrapheneOS

Description: As we become ever more reliant on our phones for most aspects of our day-to-day lives, the need for our phones' operating systems to provide guarantees of privacy and security increases. This talk will examine the ways in which law enforcement agencies, companies like Cellebrite, data brokers, and other threat actors leverage privacy flaws in "stock" Android versions. Many of these flaws are mitigated by GrapheneOS, a privacy-focused Android fork. We will discuss the technical details of these mitigations, as well as how at-risk individuals like journalists and activists can use GrapheneOS effectively to defend themselves in an increasingly surveillance-heavy digital landscape.
